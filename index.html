Beijing PM2.5 Prediction Demo

Enter air temperature, dew point, atmospheric pressure, wind direction, month and hour to get an instant PM2.5 concentration and a three-band air-quality class from gradient-boosted trees. The model is trained on hourly monitoring records from Beijing. Every metric on this page is an out-of-fold cross-validated figure, shown next to the simplest possible baseline.
